baucord

Self-hosted voice chat for you and your friends, built for the lowest-latency calls that don't sacrifice quality. Voice, screen sharing and text — no accounts, no telemetry, about as heavy as a screenshot.

Download Source on GitHub

~3.5 MB RAM  ·  Windows / macOS / Linux  ·  client + server in one zip

~/baucord v__VERSION__ 
# on whoever's hosting:
$ unzip baucord-v__VERSION__-linux-x64.zip
$ ./dedicated-server

# everyone else:
$ ./baucord # enter host:port, pick a name

# we have discord at home
$

What it does

A call with your friends: talk, type, share your screen, and control how everyone sounds on your end.

voice
Starts sending the instant you speak and stays crisp on the other end. The mic gate is yours to tune — sensitivity, volume, noise suppression.
rough connections
Each person's audio is smoothed out separately, so one friend on hotel wi-fi doesn't turn the whole call to gravel.
screen share
Share your screen with its sound. Viewers get a clean, correctly-scaled picture; you get live stats on how your stream is holding up.
per-user audio
Volume and mute per person, remembered between sessions. The loud friend stays quiet tomorrow.
global hotkeys
Mute, deafen and share from any window. Rebind by pressing the keys, not by typing arcane strings.
text chat
For links, and for whoever's mic just died.
tray & updates
Closes to the tray and keeps the call running. Checks for a new version at launch.
the server
A dumb little relay with nothing to configure and nothing to maintain. It ships in the same zip as the client.

The interface

Native widgets, one window. This is the whole app.
