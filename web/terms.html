In short

  • You must be 18 or older.

Who can use baucord

You must be at least 18 years old. Do not use baucord if you are younger. If we learn that an account on the official server belongs to a child, we delete it.

What is not allowed

On every server, in chat, direct messages, files, pictures, names, avatars, voice and screen shares:

  • Anything that sexualizes minors, including child sexual abuse material in any form. We remove it, ban the account and report it to the authorities.
  • Content or activity that is illegal where you or the server are, or that helps others break the law.
  • Threats, harassment, bullying, stalking, or encouraging someone to harm themselves.
  • Hate speech: attacking people for their race, ethnicity, religion, nationality, gender, sexual orientation, disability or similar.
  • Sharing someone's private information, or intimate images of someone, without their consent.
  • Spam, scams, malware, and pretending to be someone you are not.
  • Sharing content you do not have the right to share.
  • Getting around a ban or a block, or attacking a server, its software or the people on it.

Reporting and blocking

In the app, right-click a message or a person (on a phone, press and hold a message, or tap the person) and choose Report. The report reaches that server's moderators with the message as it was; the person is not told who reported them. Choose Block to stop seeing their messages and hearing them; they can no longer send you direct messages, and they are not told. Settings → Profile lists the people you blocked.

On the official server we review reports and act on them — removing content, muting, kicking or banning — and contact the authorities when someone is in danger or the law requires it. You can also write to support@mpw.sh. On other servers, their moderators decide.

Your content

What you post stays yours, and you are responsible for it. You let the server you post to store it and pass it on to the people you send it to, which is what the app is for. You can delete your recent messages, and your whole account (below). The privacy policy says what a server keeps and for how long.

Moderation and ending

The moderators of a server may remove content, mute people, or remove them from the server, for breaking these terms or the server's own rules. We may do the same on the official server and may end any account there that breaks these terms. You can stop using baucord at any time and delete your account on a server from the app.
